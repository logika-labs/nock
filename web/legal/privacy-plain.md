nock runs on your computer, and what it records about your project stays in your repository. We, Parallel Labs in Barcelona, receive only what you choose to send: a weekly usage report, if you said yes when nock asked; a reflection round's summary, when you confirm one; and a report you write with `nock report`, with nock's version and your platform.

The usage report is counts and categories under a random project id: never code, file paths, file or tool names, prompts, or what your decisions say. `nock usage` shows what the next one would carry, and `share = false` in `.nock/config.toml` stops them. A summary or a report holds what you saw before sending it, so read it first.

The website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites; our own server in France (Scaleway, Paris) serves it, sees your IP address to deliver the page, and keeps no log of it. It keeps one thing in your browser, and only if you turn this summary off: that choice.

Our server is in the EU (Scaleway, France). It stores reports and summaries without your IP address for up to 12 months, and its access logs, with IP addresses, for up to 30 days. nock's agents run under your own coding agent's account: what they read goes to its provider, not to us.

For a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, write to [support@logika.host](mailto:support@logika.host) with your project's id from `.nock/project.json`; we answer within a month. You can complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD) or your own.
