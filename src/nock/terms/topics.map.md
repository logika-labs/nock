`.nock/topics/map.md` says when each topic and item matters and how they bear on each other; a consultant reads it. A reflection round keeps it: triggers, not facts ("if you touch X, then Y, because Z [billing/refunds-cap]"); a rejected option with what would make it viable again. It cites items by ref in brackets (`[D7]` for an earlier decision 7); the `nock-map` check fails on one that does not exist.
