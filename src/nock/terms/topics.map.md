`.nock/shared/map.md` says when each topic and decision matters and how they bear on each other; a consultant reads it. A reflection round keeps it: triggers, not facts ("if you touch X, then Y, because Z [billing/refunds-cap]"); a rejected option with what would make it viable again. It cites decisions by ref in brackets; the `nock-map` check fails on one that does not exist.
