---
term: topics.threads
aliases: [thread, reply, message, messages, discuss]
description: The threads: what to say there, and when to say nothing
---

Each topic and item has a markdown thread, the owner's messages and the agents'. `--reply "…"` on `topics update` or `items update` adds one. The owner's message waits for one answer, their chat closed until it comes: give it (the command says `answered`). When the owner is in the session, discuss there, at most a line in the thread; away, answer briefly there; nothing if the new version shows it.
