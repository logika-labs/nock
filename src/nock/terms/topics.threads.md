---
term: topics.threads
aliases: [thread, reply, message, messages, discuss]
description: The threads: what to say there, and when to say nothing
---

Each topic and item has a markdown thread, the owner's messages and the agents'. `--reply "…"` on `topics update` or `items update` adds one. When the owner is in the session, discuss there and leave at most a line in the thread; when they are away, or told you to work in the background, answer briefly in the thread; nothing if you did what was asked and the new version shows it.
