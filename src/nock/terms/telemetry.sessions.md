---
term: telemetry.sessions
aliases: [headless, transcripts, pricing, api-cost]
description: Sessions from transcripts: interactive or headless, read on as they grow, API-equivalent cost
---

Transcripts are read on from where the last read stopped whenever telemetry is read, so a long session counts as it goes, never twice. `claude -p` runs nock did not dispatch are `headless`, kept out of the numbers and rounds. Cost is API-equivalent: tokens by kind at LiteLLM's public prices, fetched daily; an unpriced model's cost is unknown, never 0.
