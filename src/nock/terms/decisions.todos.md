---
term: decisions.todos
aliases: [todo, todos, backlog, improvement]
description: A todo: something that should be done and is not yet
---

`"status": "todo"` records something that should be done and is not yet; a possible improvement is a todo tagged `improvement`. The owner accepts it onto the backlog, or rejects it: not to be done. Every session the human starts lists the backlog. When you do one, edit it to done, saying what you did.
