---
term: decisions.elaborate
aliases: [elaborate, elaborating]
description: Elaborate: an agent deepens a decision for the owner
---

Asked to elaborate (in the studio, or `.nock/nock decisions elaborate N`), a fresh agent reads the project and deepens the decision's summary, evidence and alternatives, answering the owner's note, then edits it once. It changes nothing else; the decision shows as elaborating until it does.
