---
term: decisions.tags
aliases: [tag, tags]
description: The fixed tags and your own; near-duplicates merged
---

Tags are `important`, `technical`, `product`, `improvement` and your own: a component, a subject, lowercase. `decide` prints the tags in use and the ones it just made new, so a near-duplicate is fixed while it is cheap: `.nock/nock decisions edit '{"number": N, "tags": […]}'`. A reflection round merges tags that mean the same, keeping the more used. `logika` and `instrumentation` mark a logika round's.
