term: topics.verdicts
aliases: [verdict, accept, reject, accepted, rejected, open, awaiting]
description: The owner's verdicts, and what waits on you
---

An item is open until the owner accepts or rejects it, in `.nock/nock studio` or with `/nock-triage` (`items judge`, `topics judge`: only on their word). Accepted, it stands; rejected, rework it as a new version as the thread says (a rejected todo is not done). `topics list` starts with what waits on you: threads where the owner wrote last, rejected items. Look when you start and before you finish.
