`.nock/nock status`: whether a reflection round is due, the decisions by status and review, the rejected ones to rework, the questions the owner answered and no agent acted on yet, an unshared summary, another kind of round to offer, and whether a newer nock is out. A session the human starts is told it in short, once a day or when something new calls (`nock docs reminders`).
