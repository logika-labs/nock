description: nock status: where the project stands with nock
---

`.nock/nock status`: whether a reflection round is due, the open decisions by topic, how much waits on agents in `.nock/nock inbox` (by kind), changes in `.nock/shared/` that don't hold, an unshared summary, another kind of round to offer, and whether a newer nock is out. A session the human starts is told it in short, once a day or when something new calls (`nock docs reminders`).
