`.nock/nock status`: whether a reflection round is due, the open items by topic, what waits on agents in Topics (threads where the owner wrote last, rejected items), an unshared summary, another kind of round to offer, and whether a newer nock is out. A session the human starts is told it in short, once a day or when something new calls (`nock docs reminders`).
