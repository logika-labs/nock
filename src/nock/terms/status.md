`.nock/nock status`: whether a reflection round is due, what waits on the owner by task (proposals, reviews), how much waits on agents in `.nock/nock inbox` (by kind), changes in `.nock/shared/` that don't hold, an unshared summary, the rounds due, whether a newer nock is out, and the asking session's nudges: commits unrecorded, generated files hand-edited. A session the human starts is told it in short, once a day or when something new calls (`nock docs reminders`).
