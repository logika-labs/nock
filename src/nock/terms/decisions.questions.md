`"status": "question"` asks the owner what only they can settle, when they are not there to ask. The title is the answer you propose ("Refunds never exceed the charge"); the alternatives, the other answers. Accepted is yes; rejected is no, the note saying what holds; a note alone answers in words. Sessions list each answered question until an agent acts on it and edits it to done, saying what it did.
