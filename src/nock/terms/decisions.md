---
term: decisions
aliases: [decision, decide]
description: Record a decision as you make it: nock decide
---

Record one only when a later reader would ask "why": a choice between real alternatives the owner hasn't made, a deliberate simplification, a reading of something ambiguous, a todo. Never for the owner's explicit instruction (it is the record), routine detail, a change to a decision (edit it), or twice: `decide` refuses a title a standing one nearly has. `.nock/nock decide JSON` commits it on the main checkout; keep going meanwhile.
