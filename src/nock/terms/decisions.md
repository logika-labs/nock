---
term: decisions
aliases: [decision, decide]
description: Record a decision as you make it: nock decide
---

A decision is a non-trivial choice between ways that could each work, a deliberate simplification, or a reading of something ambiguous; not what has one obvious right way. `.nock/nock decide JSON` records it (`.nock/nock docs decisions.record`) and commits it on the main checkout, from any worktree. The owner reviews it later: keep going meanwhile. A todo, or a question for the owner, is a decision too.
