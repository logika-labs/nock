---
term: eval.preregister
aliases: [preregister, preregistration, hypothesis]
description: Write the comparison down before looking at the results
---

Before a run, write down the comparison that will decide the question — the `eval compare` query or SQL, the metric, the baseline and the difference that would matter — in the Snakefile's header or a decision item. Looking first and then choosing the cut finds a winner in noise.
