description: A decision: when to record one, and its answer, alternatives and status
---

Only when a later reader would ask "why": a choice between real alternatives the owner hasn't made, a deliberate simplification, a reading of something ambiguous, a todo you leave. Never the owner's own instruction or a routine detail. `decision.json`: `{"answer": markdown, "alternatives": [{"option", "why_not"}], "status": "done" | "todo"}`; the title says what was decided. The owner's call: the answer you propose, carried on with. Its assets show what it looks like.
