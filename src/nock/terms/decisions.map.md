`.nock/decisions/map.md` says when each decision matters and how they bear on each other; a consultant reads it. A reflection round keeps it: triggers, not facts ("if you touch X, then Y, because Z [D7]"); a rejected option with what would make it viable again; what was inferred marked *(inferred)*. It cites only standing decisions: the `nock-map` check fails on a missing or superseded one.
