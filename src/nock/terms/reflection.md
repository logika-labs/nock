term: reflection
aliases: [reflect, round, rounds]
description: A reflection round: the toolbox, the map and nock, judged and improved
---

A round is the `/nock-reflect` skill, due after `reflect.sessions` (15) sessions or `reflect.days` (7) days. `.nock/nock reflect` writes its material (again, it picks up the round under way, rewriting nothing; a later one that day is `DATE-2`); three subagents improve the toolbox, keep the map and judge nock; the lead writes `report.md` and `share.md`; `reflect finish JSON` runs nock's checks and records the round. Each change says what it should do by the next round, which holds it to that.
