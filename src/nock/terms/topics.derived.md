`{"path": FILE}` (or `{"text": …, "name": "email.md"}`), with `from` (the project files it shows), `when` (the case it shows), `recipe` (`{"command": …}` and/or `{"instruction": …}`). Images (a GIF animates), videos, PDFs, HTML and text show in the studio; over 20 MB stays local. A video takes `captions` (WebVTT; `NAME.vtt` beside it by default) and a `poster`. New version: follow the recipe, `--version`.
