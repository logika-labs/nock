description: A derived file: made to show part of the project, and how to make it again
---

`{"path": FILE}` (or `{"text": …, "name": "email.md"}`), with `from`, the project files it shows; `when`, the condition it shows ("the payment lapsed"); `recipe`, `{"command": …}` and/or `{"instruction": …}` to make it again. Images, videos, PDFs, HTML and text show in the studio; a file over 20 MB stays on this machine. Asked for another version, follow the recipe and add it with `--version`.
