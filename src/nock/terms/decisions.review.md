Done: unreviewed asks for a check; accepted, it stands; rejected, rework it. Todo: on the backlog, or not to be done. Tagged `important`, it is the owner's call, carried on with until reviewed. The owner reviews in `.nock/nock studio` or with `/nock-triage`, and the session that recorded it hears once. `review` and `note` are the owner's word: set them only when told to. A change to what a decision says asks for a fresh review, and keeps the note.
