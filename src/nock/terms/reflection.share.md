---
term: reflection.share
aliases: [share, summary]
description: nock share: a round's summary, vetted, sent on the owner's word
---

A round's `share.md` says how nock did, abstractly enough that nobody could tell the project: no names, paths, code, people or links (the `nock-share` check). `.nock/nock share` has a fresh agent vet it and shows what would be sent; with `'{"send": true}'`, only on the owner's yes, it goes to nock's makers, who alone read it. Nothing is published. With `share = false` it refuses to send.
