---
term: reflection.share
aliases: [share, summary]
description: nock share: a round's summary, vetted, sent on the owner's word
---

A round's `share.md` says how nock did, abstractly enough that nobody could tell the project: no names, paths, code, people or links (the `nock-share` check). `.nock/nock share '{"visibility": "private"}'` has a fresh agent vet it and shows what would be sent; with `"send": true`, only on the owner's yes, it goes to nock's makers (`private`) or is published on nock.dev (`public`).
