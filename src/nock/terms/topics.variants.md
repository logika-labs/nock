---
term: topics.variants
aliases: [variant, variants, prefer, preferred]
description: Variants: several takes on one asset, of which the owner prefers one
---

`"variants": true` with several files of one type (`"derived": [three takes on one picture]`) makes them variants of one asset. The card shows the preferred one, else the first; the owner stars one in the viewer (`items show` has `preferred`), and accepts the asset only once one is preferred. Never unrelated files: those are items of their own. Lay them out with a view (`docs topics.views`).
