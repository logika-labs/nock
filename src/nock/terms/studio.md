term: studio
aliases: [page, web, owner-page, chat, tidy]
description: nock studio: the owner's local page
---

`.nock/nock studio` (`--open`, `--port N`) serves the owner's page until stopped. Topics: each decision with its assets, each in a viewer. Timeline: what happened. The owner's message waits for your one answer; Address now sends an agent, or, while you wait on `nock inbox`, puts it in your inbox (so does the global chat). A bar searches or asks; Tidy up reorganizes the topics in one commit. It is the owner's: an agent starts it only when asked.
