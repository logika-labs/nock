term: studio
aliases: [page, web, owner-page]
description: nock studio: the owner's local page
---

`.nock/nock studio` (with `'{"open": true}'` or `'{"port": N}'`) serves the owner's page on 127.0.0.1 until stopped. Decisions: reviewed in place (accept, reject, note, elaborate); `#7` opens decision 7. Assets: a grid or list; one opened with any version, two compared (a text as a diff); approve, reject, note, regenerate. Evaluations: each scenario as a graph of its runs, never running a step. It is the owner's: an agent starts it only when asked.
