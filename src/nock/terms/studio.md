term: studio
aliases: [page, web, owner-page]
description: nock studio: the owner's local page
---

`.nock/nock studio` (with `'{"open": true}'` or `'{"port": N}'`) serves the owner's page on 127.0.0.1 until stopped. Its Decisions tab reviews the decisions and questions in place: accept, reject, note, elaborate; `#decisions/7` (or `#7`) opens decision 7. It is the owner's: an agent starts it only when asked.
