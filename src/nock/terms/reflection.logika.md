---
term: reflection.logika
aliases: [logika-round, nock-reflect-logika]
description: The logika round: rules and analytics for the ledger, from the data, with the owner
---

`/nock-reflect-logika` runs only when the owner asks; a session start offers it at most weekly. `.nock/nock reflect logika` writes the material of two parts: rules (each quiet on a capture, firing on a replay and on its breach planted in the code) and analytics. The session decides them, asking the owner only directions and real ambiguities, and adds the fields they need (kind `instrumentation`). `reflect logika finish JSON` records it, each rule `tested`; `logika sync` is the owner's word.
