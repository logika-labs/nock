---
term: reflection.logika
aliases: [logika-round, nock-reflect-logika]
description: The logika round: ledger rules from the data, with the owner
---

`/nock-reflect-logika` runs only when the owner asks; a session start offers it at most weekly. `.nock/nock reflect logika` writes its material. A rule lands once agreed, quiet on a real capture (`logika test`) and firing on a replay. A later question is a decision tagged `logika`; missing state, a todo tagged `logika` and `instrumentation`. `reflect logika finish JSON` records it, each rule saying how it was `tested`; `logika sync` is the owner's word.
