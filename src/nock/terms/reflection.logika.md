term: reflection.logika
aliases: [logika-round]
description: The logika rounds: rules and analytics for the ledger, from the data, with the owner
---

`logika.rules` and `logika.analytics` run only when the owner asks (`/logika` both, `/logika-rules`, `/logika-analytics`); a session start offers them when due. Rules: a part per directory, each event's risk its rule, or a reason, from `logika map`; analytics: what to measure. The session decides, asking the owner only directions and real ambiguities, adds the fields rules need, ends with one `logika replay` noise check and submits each round. `logika sync` is the owner's word.
