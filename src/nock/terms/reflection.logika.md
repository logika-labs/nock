---
term: reflection.logika
aliases: [logika-round]
description: The logika round: rules and analytics for the ledger, from the data, with the owner
---

`/nock-reflect-logika` runs only when the owner asks; a session start offers it at most weekly. `.nock/nock reflect logika` writes the material: rules (a part per directory: each event's risk its rule, or a reason, from `logika map`) and analytics. The session decides, asking the owner only directions and real ambiguities, adds the fields rules need, and ends with one `logika replay` noise check. `reflect logika finish JSON` records it; `logika sync` is the owner's word.
