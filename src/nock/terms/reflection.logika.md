---
term: reflection.logika
aliases: [logika-round, nock-reflect-logika]
description: The logika round: rules and analytics for the ledger, from the data, with the owner
---

`/nock-reflect-logika` runs only when the owner asks; a session start offers it at most weekly. `.nock/nock reflect logika` writes two parts' material: rules (what must hold, each quiet on a capture and firing on a replay, with a one-sentence `description`) and analytics (identities, dimensions, funnels, milestones, views). Missing state: a todo tagged `logika` and `instrumentation`. `reflect logika finish JSON` records it, each rule `tested`; `logika sync` is the owner's word.
