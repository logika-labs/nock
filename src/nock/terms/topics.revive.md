---
term: topics.revive
aliases: [revive, finished, again]
description: A finished topic that comes back: revived, its history kept
---

A topic whose items are all decided is finished: new items are refused. When its work comes back, revive it rather than open another: `.nock/nock topics update TOPIC --revive "why it is back"`. The note reads as its description from then on; in the studio its items show newest first, the revival a message in its chat.
