---
term: topics.revive
aliases: [revive, finished, again, reopen]
description: A done or abandoned topic that comes back: revived, its history kept
---

A done or abandoned topic takes no new items; replies, versions and verdicts on its items go on. When its work comes back, revive it rather than open another: `.nock/nock topics update TOPIC --revive "why it is back"` sets it active. The note reads as its description from then on; in the studio its items show newest first, the revival in its chat.
