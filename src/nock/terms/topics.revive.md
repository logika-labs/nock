---
term: topics.revive
aliases: [revive, finished, again]
description: A finished topic that comes back: revived, its history kept
---

A topic whose items are all decided is finished: it folds away in the studio, and new items are refused. When its work comes back, revive it rather than open another: `.nock/nock topics update TOPIC --revive "why it is back"`. The note reads as its description from then on; its page shows the new round on top, earlier ones below.
