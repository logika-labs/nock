---
term: topics.options
aliases: [variants, prefer, preferred]
description: Options: variants of one asset in one item, for the owner to prefer one
---

`"options": true` with several files of one type (`"derived": [three takes on one picture]`) makes them options: shown side by side, and the owner may mark one preferred, which is only recorded (`items show` has `preferred`: its version and name) and changes nothing else. Never unrelated files: those are items of their own. Compare in the studio is for versions, so it is off for an item with options.
