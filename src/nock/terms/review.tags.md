---
term: review.tags
aliases: [item-tags]
description: An item's tags: a topic across keys, or none
---

A tag groups items on one topic the owner would look at together across keys (`pricing`, `onboarding`), often with decisions. Never the product's or project's name, nor what the key or the kind already says: the studio shows the key and the kind (image, video, pdf, html, text), so `email` on `email/…` or `prompt` on a prompt groups nothing. Most items need none. When the project fixes its tags (`decisions.tags`), an item's come from them (`important` aside).
