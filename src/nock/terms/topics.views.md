`"views": [{"text": HTML, "name": "sizes.html", "title": "At three sizes"}]` (or `"path"`) beside an asset's files. The studio shows it as a tab of the viewer, with nock's styles (`label`, `grid`, `row`, `card`, `caption`, `checker`, `dark`, `pixels`) and `window.nock.assets` (name, type, address, preferred). Reach the files by name: `<img src="a.svg">`. Each favicon at 16, 32 and 64 px is a view.
